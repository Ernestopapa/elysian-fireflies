- Farming is a known, accepted risk under the open gate. 1/wallet limits it, it does not prevent it.

No promises in this report: no floor price, no returns, no token, no airdrop, no game, no merch. Traits are art only.

## Team 2 — Concept & art direction

### Lore: why fireflies fit Elysium
Elysium, in Greek myth, is the paradise where the blessed go after death — a place of light, calm and beauty at the end of a long journey. A firefly is a small light that only appears in the dark, and only for a short time. On launch day, 9 Nov 2026, Elysium went from an idea to a real chain, and the first lights appeared on it. The fireflies are those first lights — proof something is alive here now.
Speed: Elysium runs at about 100ms per block. A firefly's blink is fast, brief and gone, then back again — a natural picture of a chain that moves almost as fast as you can notice. The art should lean into a quick pulse of light, not a slow lamp.
Builders: Elysium is built by Kinetiq (kHYPE team), HYPE is the gas — the fuel the lights run on. The story is not about a token or a reward; it is about a new place lighting up for the first time. There will only ever be 3,333 of them.

### Art model: variants, not one shared image — RECOMMENDED
One shared image would make all 3,333 identical — owning one would feel like owning a copy. A variant system costs only a little more (one image per variant, traits in that variant's metadata) and makes a free mint worth holding and talking about. Minting is random, so nobody can pick their variant — the surprise is part of the mint.

Three axes: Glow colour, Brightness, Wings.

Background and Sparkle are tied to the variant (Aurora gets Aurora Sky + Trail; White Star gets Starfield + Many), so each of the 12 images is distinct and each metadata file is honest about what the image shows. No trait unlocks anything — no points, no future reward.

### Why 1-per-wallet is collectible (vs Blurb's 5/wallet)
Blurb let one wallet mint 5, so one person could hold a stack and the collection felt like a pile. One per wallet changes the feeling: your firefly is *the one you got*. You cannot mint again to chase a better colour, and you cannot hold five at once — if you want a different glow, you trade, or you accept the one the dark gave you. That is the same model that worked for Seven Sigma (166 holders, each exactly one). The variant system stops "one each" from feeling like "all the same": with 12 variants, two strangers comparing fireflies will almost always have different ones. A Legendary Aurora (50 exist) is genuinely rare; a Common Meadow Gold is still *your* Meadow Gold, the only one your wallet will ever mint.

### Launch hook — one line (not a full draft)
> The first lights on Elysium — 3,333 free fireflies, one per wallet, gone when they're gone.
Free, first, limited, one each. The reason to mint on day 1 is that this is a launch-day object — the lights that were there when the chain switched on — and once 3,333 wallets have claimed, there are no more.

## Team 3 — Launch viability & risks

### Economics (estimates — verify live at deploy time)
Gas is the small fee paid to the chain for doing work, paid in HYPE on Elysium. The mint itself is $0 — the minter only pays gas.
- Gas price seen 9 Nov 2026: ~0.149 gwei (one snapshot, not a guarantee). HYPE ~$73–$75 that day ($75.24 at 17:07 UTC). Dollar figures move with HYPE — do not quote as fixed.
- Mainnet deploy: a standard OpenZeppelin ERC-721 typically uses 2.5–4.0M gas. At 0.149 gwei: low 2.5M gas = 0.00037 HYPE (~$0.027–$0.028); mid 3M = 0.00045 HYPE (~$0.033–$0.034); high 4M = 0.00060 HYPE (~$0.044–$0.045). Sources: https://23stud.io/blog/smart-contract-platform-economics-cost-comparison-2025 · https://altcoininvestor.com/how-to-create-an-nft-for-free/
- Caveats: the Fireflies contract does not exist yet — real gas is only known after compile + testnet estimate. Elysium is an Arbitrum Orbit chain: a plain HYPE transfer uses ~21,140 gas, not 21,000, "because Nitro adds the cost of posting the transaction to HyperEVM" (Alchemy) — that posting surcharge applies to deploys too and is not fully captured above. Gas can spike: at 10× (1.49 gwei) a 3M-gas deploy is 0.00447 HYPE (~$0.34); at 10 gwei it is 0.03 HYPE (~$2.25). Still small.
- Minter gas per free mint: ~100,000–200,000 gas → 0.0000149–0.0000298 HYPE → ~$0.001–$0.002 at the seen price. The real cost to a minter is getting HYPE onto Elysium, not the mint gas.
- `setBaseURI` ~50,000 gas ≈ 0.0000075 HYPE ≈ $0.0006. Verification on Sourcify / explorer costs no gas.
- Bridge in (HyperEVM → Elysium): official bridge, HYPE 1:1, deposits "typically land within a minute" (Alchemy; Mesa: "usually within a minute"). No bridge platform fee stated; HyperEVM-side gas unverified.
- Bridge out (Elysium → HyperEVM): ~5.5–7 days challenge period + a claim transaction. Sources: Pangea ("about 5.5 days"), Mesa ("about 6 to 7 days"), Synthra ("about 5.6 days"). HYPE bridged in cannot be quickly pulled back out.
- Testnet: $0 (faucet HYPE).
- **Reserve recommendation: bridge 0.5 HYPE (~$36–$38 at $73–$75), as SPECS.md §5 already suggests — do not bridge more.** That is ~1,100× the mid deploy estimate, covering deploy, `setBaseURI`, retries and a large gas spike. 0.2 HYPE would still cover ~440× base cost; 0.5 avoids a second bridge trip. Do not bridge 1+ HYPE "just in case" — the extra sits locked on Elysium for ~6 days if it needs to come back. Deployer wallet (throwaway, gas only) holds the bridged HYPE; user wallet stays owner; leftover deployer HYPE is returned after deploy, with the ~6-day withdrawal wait noted.

### Discovery: how a free open mint gets found
There is no single "new mints" feed that guarantees eyeballs. Realistic path:
1. Contract real and checkable: deploy → verify on Sourcify + Elysium explorer → `tokenURI(1)` shows art live. Without this, marketplaces hide broken metadata and nobody serious mints an unverified contract.
2. Mint site live (Cloudflare Pages, professional, EN / 中文, paste-address checker main flow).
3. X post by the user, manually (no X login). The main discovery engine. It must explain in one screen: free, 1/wallet, chain Elysium, gas only in HYPE, how to add the chain and bridge, and the contract link. Most of the user's existing audience is on BSC and has no HYPE on Elysium.
4. ElysiumEco listing request (https://elysiumeco.xyz/, community tracker, not official). Submit with contract, site, X links. Expect a request, not a listing.
5. Explorer visibility: lets someone who already knows the address verify it. It does not bring strangers.
6. OpenSea indexing: "will be supporting Elysium from day one" (Alchemy; Kismo repeats it). That is chain-level support, not a promise *this* collection appears quickly. On established chains a standard ERC-721 is typically indexed within minutes, but on a brand-new chain delay of hours to a day is plausible. **Do not link OpenSea until the collection page actually loads.** Check Kalos as a secondary venue, only after it loads the collection.
7. Second-wave discovery: if the mint moves, ecosystem round-ups, ElysiumEco quests/leaderboard and other lists pick it up. If it does not move in the first 48 hours, this step does not happen on its own.

Not guaranteed: OpenSea indexing speed or at all for a new collection on a day-one chain; ElysiumEco listing; any Elysium-official promotion (no official token/airdrop/points — any "ELY" claim is a scam); BSC audience converting (HYPE → HyperEVM → bridge → add chain 99901 → mint is real friction).

### Farming / sybil risk (open gate + 1/wallet)
Honest headline: **1/wallet is a limit, not sybil-proof. It stops one wallet taking hundreds. It does not stop one person using hundreds of wallets.**
- It caps damage per wallet at 1. With 1/wallet, mints ≈ unique holders by construction — the Sigma proof (166 holders, each exactly 1).
- It does not raise the cost of a new identity: wallet addresses are free to generate; the only per-wallet cost is gas.
- What a farmer would do: generate 50–500 fresh wallets (free, seconds), fund each with ~0.00003 HYPE for one mint, script `freeMint()` from each. At ~$0.001–$0.002 gas per mint, **100 farmed mints cost roughly $0.10–$0.20 in mint gas**, plus funding-transfer gas. EOA-only blocks a bulk-mint contract, not a script driving many normal wallets.
- The thing that keeps farming low at launch is that there is nothing to farm *for*: no token, no airdrop, no paid phase, no promised future benefit. Farming only pays if secondary demand appears.
- Mitigations that do NOT add a gate (locked open gate, no allowlist, no mint-site gate): max 1 per tx, no bulk-mint function; EOA-only; no short mint window that rewards speed alone (open-ended mint lets real users arrive late); monitor holders and publish the truth (how many funded from the same source, minted within seconds, transferred to one wallet within an hour); do not reward minting with anything else (no points, no snapshot promise); do not add site-level friction without a fresh user decision (it slows real users too and conflicts with the no-gates rule).
- What would actually stop farming: only identity (allowlist, already rejected) or a cost per mint high enough to make it unprofitable. Posture: accept farming as a known risk, limit it to 1/wallet, monitor it, do not create incentives that make it pay.

### Flop vs success
Blurb (closest comparison, and a warning): 3,333 free, HyperEVM, 5/wallet, with a "$BLURB airdrop per NFT" promise — the one thing Fireflies will *not* copy. Checked 9 Nov ~18:25 GMT: 263/3,333 = 7.9%. Even with a token promise, under 8% minted. Free alone does not sell. Unverified: Blurb's launch date/time, so no honest mints-per-day rate can be calculated. Fireflies is stricter in ways that hurt discovery (1/wallet not 5, no token promise, new chain not HyperEVM where the audience already is) and stricter in ways that help quality (1/wallet, no token promise to farm for).
Elysium × OpenSea mint (8,888, free, 1/wallet, 10–11 Nov — dates unverified, Outposts page body empty on open): eligibility is **gated, not open** (Hypurr NFT, HYPE genesis allocation, >10,000 KNTQ, kPoints, select OpenSea mobile users, bought KNTQ on OpenSea, >$100K Markets.xyz volume, >1 kHYPE). What could make Fireflies flop: (1) no reason to mint beyond "it's free"; (2) bridge friction kills the BSC audience; (3) launch-day noise — official 8,888 drop, competing free mints (Slymz, TORPOR, erupt, Pixel Blobs, Kismo), 1,124 new Ascend coins in 24h; (4) OpenSea does not index in time; (5) a technical stall on day one (RPC down, bridge slow, metadata not loading, site failing on mobile); (6) farming takes the visible supply first.
